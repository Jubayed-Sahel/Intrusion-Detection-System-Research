# Controlled LIME kernel-width sensitivity protocol

**Frozen before computation:** 8 November 2026 (Asia/Dhaka). This is an exploratory follow-up after seeing the frozen primary and LIME budget results. The width grid, endpoint and tests are fixed before running this extension. This is not a search for a best width and no result selects a setting for a new test.

## Matched design

- Use the same eight saved seed-42 classifiers, training-fitted preprocessing and 100 diagnostic test rows per context as the completed budget study. Verify input and checkpoint hashes, saved feature order, row/class identity, original target probability, and training background indices. The background uses at most 5,000 rows stratified by the classifier's training predictions with seed 42, exactly as in the budget study. Classifiers are not refitted.
- Compare kernel widths 0.15, 0.35, and 0.75 times the square root of the prepared predictor count at 3,500 perturbations. The 0.75 width is the library default and the frozen budget-study reference. Keep all other LIME settings, including categorical handling, continuous discretization, and per-record seed, fixed. Use a fresh explainer at each width. Within a record verify exact equality of generated classifier-input neighborhoods across the three widths and share their classifier predictions to save work. Kernel weighting is the only changed setting.
- Replay all 800 default-width explanations and verify their coefficients, weighted R², local predictions and per-budget probability drops against the frozen budget outputs to numerical tolerance (relative 1e-9, absolute 1e-12). Verify that a default constructor actually uses the 0.75 width. Store the verified baseline alongside both new widths and never overwrite the budget study. Save record-level fit and deletion, coefficients, frozen dependencies, checkpoint hashes and summary tables.
- Explain only the fitted classifier's original predicted-class probability. Verify the requested output-column index, class label and original query prediction. Under this dense, fully discretized LIME design the interpretable query vector is all ones: verify categorical indices cover every feature, scaler centering/scaling is 0/1, and the target-specific intercept plus summed coefficients reproduces `local_pred`. Retain the intercept and the target probability. Surrogate predictions are unbounded linear predictions; absolute point errors are not clipped.

## Endpoint and inference

Rank by absolute attribution. Evaluate the frozen training median/mode replacement at budgets 1, 3, 5 and 10 on the unchanged classifier, measuring the original predicted-class probability drop. Reuse the original saved TreeSHAP rankings and verify their drops against the budget study. Average records within connection and budget, then budgets, to give each explained connection equal weight. Report all widths in every context. Do not treat the local fit scores as deletion metrics: in-sample weighted R² values at different widths emphasize different parts of the same simulated neighborhood. Point error compares the local surrogate against the classifier at the same original query; it does not establish broader local fidelity.

Declare exactly 16 new two-sided contrasts: saved TreeSHAP minus LIME-3500 at each of the two new widths in each of eight contexts. Use exact sign tests excluding differences whose absolute value is at most 1e-12 and Holm adjustment over these 16 tests. Report group-weighted mean differences and 95% pointwise percentile intervals from 2,000 paired connection bootstrap resamples. The default-width comparison remains in the budget study's own family; do not mix its adjusted p values with this family. The new sign tests concern direction frequency among non-ties, not the mean. Source-campaign dependence may remain. No independent capture, causal validation, automatic choice of width or runtime comparison is implied.
